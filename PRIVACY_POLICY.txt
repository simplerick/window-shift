WindowShift Privacy Policy  
Effective date: October 27, 2026


1. WindowShift Application

WindowShift does not collect, transmit, or store personal data, usage statistics, analytics, or telemetry.

The application operates locally on your device and does not require an account.

2. Website

The WindowShift website may collect limited technical information necessary to operate, protect, and understand the usage of the website.

The website uses Cloudflare services, including web analytics. Cloudflare may process technical information associated with requests to the website in accordance with its own privacy policy.

WindowShift does not use this information to identify individual visitors.

3. Third-Party Services

The website may contain links to third-party websites, including GitHub. Those websites are governed by their own privacy policies and practices.

4. Changes

This Privacy Policy may change in the future. The current version will be published on this page.
